---
title: Joining the Django Sprint at PyCon Korea 2026
---

On August 17, PyLadies Seoul joined the Django sprint at PyCon Korea 2026. The session covered contributing to open source, from exploring issues in Django and third-party projects to writing and reviewing pull requests.

It was a chance to explore how we can help build the tools we use. Thank you to the sprint facilitators and everyone who took part!
