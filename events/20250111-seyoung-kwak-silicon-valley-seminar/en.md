💡 **2025's First Seminar with Seyoung Kwak, Silicon Valley Backend Developer**

Hello! PyLadies Seoul is back with a web seminar for 2025!
We are thrilled to have Seyoung Kwak as our speaker. She has been walking the path of a developer in Silicon Valley for about 10 years as a woman and an Asian, and is the author of the essay "**I Thought Everything Would Be Fine Once I Got Here**".

Seyoung will share her journey of becoming a developer in the U.S., her experiences in Silicon Valley, and valuable tips for working abroad.
