---
title: Presenting Our Poster at PyCon Taiwan 2026
---

Delighted to present our poster representing **PyLadies Seoul** at **PyCon Taiwan 2026**!

A huge thank you to Soojin Yoon, Hwayoung Cha, Jiho Park, and Yeonwoo Choi, our amazing Taiwan delegation. We all worked so hard, and I'm so glad we pulled this off together!

Special thanks to Wun Hua Ke and the organizers for putting together such a wonderful and welcoming event. From PyRun to PyNight, every moment made this visit truly unforgettable.

It was a pleasure connecting with the Python community in Taiwan. Can't wait to reconnect with everyone at **PyCon Hong Kong** and **PyLadies Asia**!
