**<Introduction>**
In an era where AI writes code, is overseas employment still a viable path? With anxiety growing about generative AI eroding developers' standing, many of you may be pausing your dreams of working abroad.
But the view from the field may be a little different. For this PyLadies seminar, we invited Jihyun Eom, author of the bestselling 《I'm a Developer, Living in London》 and a developer working in the UK.
She'll share the real reality of the job market she experienced firsthand in London — not vague fantasies. What kind of talent are UK companies looking for amid shifting tech trends, and what should we prepare? Come hear her live insights.

**<Recommended for>**

* Anyone vaguely dreaming of working abroad (especially in the UK/Europe)
* Anyone reflecting on their career direction as a developer amid AI advances
* Anyone curious about the hiring culture and ideal candidate profile at overseas Big Tech
* Anyone who enjoyed 《I'm a Developer, Living in London》 and wants to talk to the author

**<What you'll learn>**

* The current vibe of the UK IT job market from someone on the inside
* In the AI era, what skills and attitudes global companies look for in developers
* How a Korean developer adapted and grew in London
* Motivation and mindset
