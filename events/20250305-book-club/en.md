PyLadies Seoul is excited to announce our Monthly Book Club for 2025!
📚 The Monthly Book Club is a gathering where we read one book each month and meet online on the first Wednesday evening to share our thoughts.

This month's book is "Don't Try to Draw Well" by Naoki Saito, a popular illustrator YouTuber with 800,000 subscribers. Although it's a book about illustration, we chose it because its insights can be valuable for developers too!

**Book Introduction**: *This book shares not only the author's thoughts and actions about becoming an illustrator that gained significant attention on YouTube but also his firsthand experience and expertise as a professional illustrator. Using a Q&A format with detailed examples, he addresses common concerns like "I can't draw well," "People don't look at my work," "I'm not getting any jobs," and "I lack motivation." The book uses illustrations and simple diagrams to explain key points more clearly. It also discusses the mindset creators should maintain, which can be particularly helpful when facing creative blocks.* *(Source: Kyobo Book)*

You don't need to finish the entire book to participate. Just prepare these few things for our discussion 😄

* Memorable passages you'd like to share from the book
* Stories or questions you'd like to discuss
