**September Book Club Invitation: 『Modern Elegant Development(요즘 우아한 개발)』 - "Plan, develop, and retrospect. The real development stories from Woowa Brothers”**

For our September Book Club, we'll be reading 『Modern Elegant Development (요즘 우아한 개발)』 together, which covers onboarding, development, culture, and issue management as experienced during Woowa Brothers' growth journey. This book is accessible to developers of all backgrounds and offers insights into Woowa Brothers' work culture.

**👥Recommended for:**

* Problem-solvers interested in building healthy dev cultures
* Those growing with expanding organizations and adapting to change
* People exploring stories beyond tech and sharing great books

✏️Don't feel pressured! It's okay if you don't finish the entire book. Just prepare the following few things to join our discussion:

* A comfortable and open mind
* Impressive passages from the book you'd like to share
* Stories, experiences, and interests you'd like to discuss
