title: Pie-Baking Workshop for Women Speakers
---

A special workshop for women speakers is being held in preparation for PyCon Korea 2025!
This workshop has been organized to encourage and support female developers' participation and presentations at the conference.

Date and Venue:
Location: Heyground Bricks 3rd floor, Seoul Forest Room 07

* 1st Session: Saturday, April 12, 13:00 - 15:00
* Introduction to PyCon and lowering the barrier for presentations
* 2nd Session: Saturday, April 19, 13:00 - 15:00
* Individual lightning talk presentations and feedback
* 3rd Session: Prepare presentations, receive feedback, and submit CFP (online)

Workshop Contents:

* Introduction to PyCon and how to participate
* Selecting presentation topics and sharing ideas
* Tips and practice for improving presentation skills

Target Participants:

* Female engineers interested in Python
* Those who want to challenge themselves to speak at conferences
* Those who want to participate more actively in the tech community

Expected Outcomes:

* Overcoming fear of public speaking
* Forming a network of female developers
* Increasing the number of female speakers at PyCon Korea 2025

How to Apply:
Participation fee: 10,000 KRW (Toss Bank 1001-0090-0506 Yoon Soojin)

Please contact us if you have difficulty paying the fee.
Limited to 10 participants on a first-come, first-served basis.
Share your story at PyCon Korea 2025!
