**Are You Afraid AI Will Replace Your Code?**
**Learn How to Become an 'Irreplaceable Developer' from Cheuk Ting Ho, JetBrains AI Developer Advocate**

In an era where AI writes code, how is the developer's role evolving?
Join us for insights from Cheuk Ting Ho, JetBrains AI Developer Advocate and Python Software Foundation Director! Learn to clearly distinguish what AI excels at versus what it still lacks, discover real-world examples of how developers leverage AI, and explore why women developers have unique advantages in the AI era.
Developers of all levels and AI experience are welcome.
All women are welcome!

**\| Topics covered**
\- What AI is really good at vs\. what it still struggles with
\- How developers actually use AI in their coding workflow
\- How to become a better developer in the AI era
\- Why women developers have advantages in the AI landscape
