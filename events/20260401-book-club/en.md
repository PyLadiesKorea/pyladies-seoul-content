**About PyLadies Book Club**
PyLadies Seoul is the Seoul chapter of an international mentorship group dedicated to helping more women thrive in the Python community.
The PyLadies Book Club meets every first Wednesday evening of the month to read a selected book together, share insights, and enjoy networking.

**April's Book — 『파이썬 업무 자동화 일잘러 되기 + 챗GPT』**
This book gently lowers the barrier for anyone who's thought, "Is it too late for me to learn coding?"
It covers practical automation ideas using Python basics and ChatGPT to cut down on repetitive tasks and help you focus on work that actually matters. You don't need to build a perfect program right away — even complete beginners are welcome to join the conversation.

**Recommended if you:**
* Want to break free from repetitive, manual tasks
* Are interested in applying Python or generative AI to your work
* Want to focus on more meaningful, high-value work

**You don't have to finish the book!**
Just come with these three things in mind:
* A repetitive task in your work or studies you'd love to automate
* A technique or passage from the book you'd want to use right away
* A program you've always wanted to build someday
