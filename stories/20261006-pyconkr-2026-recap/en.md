---
title: Talks, Our Booth, and PyDrink at PyCon KR 2026
---

PyLadies Seoul joined PyCon KR 2026 at Dongguk University on August 15–16. On the first day, organizer Soojin Yoon gave a talk about growing together with PyLadies Seoul. She shared why we restarted the community and how our regular gatherings have connected learning, speaking, and open-source contribution.

We also ran our community booth and hosted PyDrink on both conference days, making time for conversation over drinks. From the stage to the booth and these informal gatherings, we had several ways to introduce PyLadies Seoul. Thank you to everyone who joined us and everyone who helped prepare and run the booth and activities!

You can revisit the talk through the [session page](https://2026.pycon.kr/presentations/077c5c22-fc2d-482f-8855-ff5cc0b74957), [slides](https://pyladies-slides.pages.dev/1?view=pyconkr-en&v=516ff9a0), and [recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4A8QtEaV5do).
