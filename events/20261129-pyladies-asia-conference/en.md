## It's OK not to be perfect.

PyLadies from Korea, Japan, Indonesia, and Taiwan come together in Seoul to share experiences in Python, technology, community, and careers. Join us to learn and connect through a keynote, talks, and tutorials.

## Event details

- Date: Sunday, November 29, 2026, 10:30 AM to 5:30 PM KST
- Venue: AWS, 18F, Centerfield, Yeoksam, Seoul
- Address: 231 Teheran-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
- Hosted by: PyLadies Seoul
- Open to everyone, regardless of gender
- General admission: KRW 20,000
- Students and job seekers: Free
- Lunch: A burger or salad bowl is provided for every attendee

## Program

Explore generative AI, data visualization, open source, community operations, computer vision, gRPC, and more. See the [official timetable](https://pyladies.kr/en/#timetable) for session and tutorial details.
