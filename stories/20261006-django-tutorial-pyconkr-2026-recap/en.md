---
title: Building a Django Blog Together at PyCon Korea 2026
---

On August 17, PyLadies Seoul organizers Yoon Soojin and Cha Hwayoung led a Django tutorial at PyCon Korea 2026. Using the Django Girls Tutorial, we worked through building a blog app and explored web development with Python.

A familiar idea—a blog—gave us a starting point for getting to know Django. Thank you to everyone who joined us! We hope the workshop helps you take the first steps toward a small web project of your own.
