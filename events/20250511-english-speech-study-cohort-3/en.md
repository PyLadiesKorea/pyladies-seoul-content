**PyLadies Seoul English Interview Study Group: Cohort 3**
We’re starting the 3rd round of our English interview study group!
This is an 8-week intensive program for women who are preparing for domestic or international job interviews, or simply want to improve their English speaking skills.
We’ll practice mock interviews, give short presentations, and exchange feedback to grow together.
• **Who can join**: Women interested in English interviews, overseas employment, or improving presentation skills
• **Period**: 8 weeks starting from Sunday, May 11, 2025
• **Capacity**: Up to 6 participants (first come, first served)
• **Schedule & Format**:
• Online and offline sessions will alternate every week
• Online session – Every other Sunday at 8:00 PM (Discord)
• Offline session – Every other Saturday at 10:00 AM (tentatively at the National Library of Korea)
• Each session will last about 2 hours
**Session Structure**
• **Warm-up (15 minutes)**
A small talk session to reflect on your week
• **Main Practice**
① **Mock Interview (15 minutes per person)**
• 2 soft skills + 2 technical questions (you can prepare more if you'd like)
• Follow-up questions
• Peer feedback
② **Presentation Session (5–10 minutes)**
• Topics may include portfolio, algorithms, system design, etc.
• Followed by Q&A and feedback
• Recording optional
**Weekly Tasks**
• Prepare 4 interview questions (2 soft skills + 2 technical)
• The 2 soft skills questions will be shared across all members
• The 2 technical questions can be different for each person depending on your field
• Prepare for the weekly presentation
• (Optional) Share your resume and give feedback to your assigned partner
In the final week, we’ll conduct mock interviews using 3–4 of the most challenging questions you faced during the study.
**Eligibility**: Open to all women
**Deposit**: 20,000 KRW
• 100% refundable if you attend all sessions
• 2,500 KRW will be deducted for each missed session
• If you're unable to pay the deposit, please contact us at [seoul@pyladies.com](mailto:seoul@pyladies.com)
We look forward to meeting participants who want to grow together!
