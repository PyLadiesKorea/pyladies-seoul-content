🌱 **PyLadies Seoul 2025 New Year's Resolution Sharing & Accountability Group**

Join us as PyLadies Seoul kicks off 2025 with a special initiative to help you achieve your New Year's resolutions!
At our end-of-year party in 2024, we didn't have enough time to share our goals for the new year. Now, we're creating a dedicated space not just to share these resolutions, but to support each other throughout the year in achieving them. While it's easy to give up on resolutions when you're alone, having the encouragement of PyLadies by your side can help you stay committed! 😊
Why not join the women developers of PyLadies Seoul in sharing your New Year's resolutions and turning those aspirations into reality?

**Note: This event is exclusively for women participants!**

* **Date & Time**: Thursday, January 23, 2025, 7:30 PM - 8:30 PM KST
* **Location**: Private voice channel (#2025-new-year-goals) on PyLadies Seoul Discord server
* Once you join the Discord server, you'll be granted access to the private channel based on the nickname you provided in your RSVP
* Please join the Discord server by 6:00 PM on Wednesday, January 22 (the day before our first meeting) to ensure smooth access
* **Who Can Join**: Open to all women
* **Participation Fee**: 5,000 KRW (Toss Bank: 1001-0090-0506, Recipient: Yoon Sujin)
* While we'll have regular meetings throughout the year, you only need to pay this fee once!
* If you're unable to pay the fee, please contact us at seoul@pyladies.com

**Regular Check-in Meetings**

* **Schedule**: Last Thursday of every other month, 7:30 PM - 8:30 PM KST
* Meeting dates: March 27, May 29, July 31, September 25, November 27
* **Location**: Same Discord channel as the first meeting

We welcome all women who want to share their growth journey and personal goals for the new year. Whether you're just starting your tech journey or you're an experienced developer, this is a space for you to connect, share, and grow together.

Want to join us or have questions? Feel free to reach out anytime at seoul@pyladies.com - we'd love to hear from you!

Join us in making 2025 a year of achievement and growth! 🚀
