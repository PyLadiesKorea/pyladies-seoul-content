PyLadies is an international mentorship group dedicated to helping more women become active participants and leaders in the Python open-source community. **PyLadies Seoul** is the Seoul chapter, building a network where women developers can grow together.

📚 **PyLadies Book Club** We meet online on the first Wednesday evening of every month to read and discuss a selected book. We cover a wide range of topics—technology, career, and work-life balance—sharing insights and enjoying networking time together.

📖 **February Book:** *Incantations on the Commute* (출근길의 주문) *Subtitle: Practical strategies for women to protect themselves and work with confidence on an uneven playing field.*
This month, we selected a book that discusses how to stand firm and protect yourself while working in a world that often feels like a "tilted playing field" for women. Instead of vague anxiety, the author equips us with the weapons of **speaking, writing, and networking**, offering the courage to work more solidly.

📖 **About the Book**
Lee Da-hye is an author who never stops speaking and writing about women's rights and lives. Written specifically for working women, *Incantations on the Commute* is filled with the insightful commentary and empathetic prose she has shown through her books and broadcasts. For women who worry about "how long I can keep working," who have tried hard not to be pushed aside in male-dominated networks, and who work diligently but still carry vague anxieties—this book places the precise tools of "speech, writing, and networking" in your hands and awakens your fighting spirit. Armed with concrete methods you can put into practice immediately, this book will make your commute tomorrow feel more confident. *(Source: Kyobo Book Centre)*

📍 **Recommended for:**

* Professionals who want to share and discuss workplace concerns.
* Those looking to find their own language and balance while working.
* Those who want to build solidarity with female colleagues and sustain a long, healthy career.
* Those who want to cultivate the power to speak their thoughts clearly.

📍 **Don't feel pressured! It's okay if you haven't finished the book.** To join the conversation, you only need to bring a few things:

* A comfortable mindset.
* A sentence or paragraph that left an impression on you.
* A topic or experience you’d like to share.
