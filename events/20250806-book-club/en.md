**August Book Club Invitation: "A Prompt Engineer's Work Journal" - "Real-world Stories of Prompt Engineering from Korea's First Prompt Engineer"**

Join us for our August Book Club as we read "A Prompt Engineer's Work Journal," which contains the know-how and experience of Korea's first prompt engineer. This book covers the principles of prompt engineering for effective communication with AI and real-world practical experience.

\

**👥Recommended for:**

* Those who want to use AI tools more efficiently
* Developers who want to become familiar with prompt engineering
* Anyone who simply wants to read a good book together and share discussions

* A comfortable and open mind
* Impressive passages from the book you'd like to share
* Stories, experiences, and interests you'd like to discuss
