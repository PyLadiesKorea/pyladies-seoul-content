title: Careers, Team Leadership, and AI with Sooyoung Moon
---

On September 5, we welcomed Sooyoung Moon, AI Pack Team Lead at Upstage, for a seminar about her journey from biology to software team leadership and the things she learned by trying.

We heard about her career transition, her experience leading a team, and her experiments with AI at work. After the talk, we continued the conversation through Q&A and networking.

Thank you to Sooyoung for sharing her experience, and to everyone who joined us. We look forward to making more room for conversations about the different paths and experiments that shape our careers.
