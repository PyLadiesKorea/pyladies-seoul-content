# **July Book Club Announcement:**

**“A Liberal Arts Woman in an IT Company”**
*“A liberal arts graduate’s perspective on working in an IT company and stories of growth.”*
This month, our book club will read **“A Liberal Arts Woman in an IT Company”**—a book that shares the experiences and perspectives of a non-technical (liberal arts) graduate navigating the world of IT.
The book vividly portrays the challenges and growth of a non-major in the tech industry, as well as the importance of collaboration and communication.

***

## **Who Should Join?**

* **Non-majors interested in working in IT**
* **Anyone looking to grow in a diverse workplace**
* **Those curious about communication and teamwork in tech**
* **Anyone who wants to learn about real-life experiences in IT companies**

***

Join us in July as we read **“A Liberal Arts Woman in an IT Company”** and share diverse perspectives and stories!
Through this book, you’ll discover new viewpoints and inspiring growth stories from the IT industry.
