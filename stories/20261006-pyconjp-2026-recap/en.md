---
title: Bringing Seoul's Story to PyCon JP 2026 in Hiroshima
---

On August 22, PyLadies Seoul organizer Soojin Yoon joined the invited session “PyLadies: Growing Communities Around the World” at PyCon JP 2026 in Hiroshima. Alongside Maaya Ishida of PyLadies Tokyo and Carol Willing of PyLadies San Diego, she shared community experiences from Seoul, Tokyo, and San Diego.

The conversation explored finding fellow organizers, keeping gatherings going alongside busy lives, and creating a space where newcomers feel welcome. It was meaningful to share our work in Seoul with PyLadies from other cities. Thank you to PyCon JP for bringing us together, and to Maaya and Carol for sharing their experiences!

Learn more on the [official session page](https://2026.pycon.jp/en/talks/GNX9Y9) and in [PyCon JP's introduction to the invited session](https://pyconjp.blogspot.com/2026/08/en-pyconjp2026-invitedtalk.html?m=1).
