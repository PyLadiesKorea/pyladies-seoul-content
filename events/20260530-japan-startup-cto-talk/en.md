**[Find Your Own Rhythm, Not the Correct Answer]**
**From an n-Year Non-Tech Background to a CTO at a Japanese Startup**
We have prepared a very special seminar for those who want to build their careers at their own pace and rhythm, rather than following the crowd.
Even with a degree in computer science, starting out as a developer wasn’t immediate, leading to years spent in non-tech roles. "Is it too late to start over?" Amidst countless moments of doubt and hesitation, Kim Doyoon embraced his background as a former publishing editor, using that broad perspective and unique experience to shape his own distinct "vibe." Today, he is thriving as the CTO of hundreds LLC, a startup in Japan. In this seminar, he will share his vivid story of building a global career.

If you are curious about how to survive and thrive flexibly in the real world with a macro perspective—rather than getting trapped in technical perfectionism—and how to grow on the global stage through "Vibe Coding," don't miss out on this meetup!

**[This seminar is perfect for you if:]**
• You want to restart or transition into a developer career but feel stuck.
• You are wondering how to turn your past experience in non-tech roles into a powerful weapon in tech.
• You are curious about surviving as a developer in Japan's IT industry and global environments.
• You want to learn how to work with "Vibe Coding" while understanding the entire context of a service.
