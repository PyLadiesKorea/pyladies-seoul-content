PyLadies is an international mentorship group helping more women take part in the Python community. PyLadies Seoul is the Seoul chapter, building a network where women developers can grow.
📚 PyLadies Book Club meets every first Wednesday evening of the month, reading a selected book together and discussing it online. It's a space to share insights through technical reading and enjoy networking.

**December Book Club: 『I'm a Developer, Living in London』 (개발자인데요, 런던 살아요)**
For December's book club we're reading 『I'm a Developer, Living in London』, which captures a Korean developer's journey starting a new career and life in London. The author, who was facing a slump and wanting change, moved to the UK, took a job at a local IT company, and the book is an essay-style account of daily life and growth there. It resonates with anyone wrestling with similar questions, interested in an overseas career, or just thinking about the direction of their own life.

<The book covers the past and present of an author living in London. Despite having a career most would envy, she fell into a slump — and the book tells us how she ended up in the UK, why the UK, and what her life looks like now. Curiously, the author's past and present make us dream of our own future. Reading her story and picturing the landscapes she sees, we might already find ourselves holding a plane ticket to the UK. The book is divided into three parts: the job-finding journey leaving Korea, working in a UK company and building relationships with people there, and the small everyday moments of life as a working professional in the UK. May any page light a small spark in your heart.> (Source: Kyobo Book)

👥 **Recommended for**

* Developers thinking about post-slump career changes, working abroad, or emigration
* Office workers interested in the realities and culture of the IT industry
* Anyone wanting to adapt to and grow in a new country
* Anyone who needs "change" or "challenge" in their life

✏️ No pressure! You don't have to finish the book. Just bring a few things for the conversation:

* A relaxed, open mind
* Memorable passages you'd like to share after reading
* Stories, experiences, and interests you'd like to talk about
