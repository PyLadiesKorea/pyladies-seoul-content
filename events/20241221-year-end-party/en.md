**PyLadies Seoul Year-End Party & First Anniversary Celebration 2024!**

We're excited to host a special year-end party to reflect on the meaningful moments of 2024 with PyLadies Seoul. This December is particularly special as it marks PyLadies Seoul's first anniversary! We're creating a space for participants to look back on their 2024 journey and network with other Python enthusiasts.

Important Note: This event is exclusively for women participants.

Event Details:
* **Date & Time:** Saturday, December 21st, 2024, 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM
* **Venue:** Wishket Office, 3rd Floor, Korea Foundation for Advanced Studies Building (Venue sponsored by Wishket)
* ***Important Notice: There are cats at the party venue. Please take note if you have allergies or are afraid of cats!***

Who Should Attend:
* Open to all women
* Especially recommended for:
* Anyone interested in networking with Python enthusiasts
* Those curious about PyLadies Seoul's activities throughout 2024
* Pizza lovers!

What to Bring:
* A small gift (value under 10,000 KRW) for gift exchange

Participation Fee:
* 15,000 KRW (Toss Bank: 1001-0090-0506, Yoon Sujin)
* The fee covers refreshments and event preparations
* If you're having difficulty with the participation fee, please contact us at seoul@pyladies.com

Schedule:
* **3:00 - 3:30 PM** PyLadies Seoul Introduction & Ice Breaking
* **3:30 - 4:00 PM** Let's Get to Know Each Other! Games
* **4:00 - 5:00 PM** Sharing Our 2024 Retrospectives
* **5:00 PM onwards** Pizza Party & Networking (Optional attendance!)

While not all participants may be fluent in English, some attendees and organizers can communicate in English!
For any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at seoul@pyladies.com.
Thank you, and we look forward to seeing you!
